seedColor: Colors.green

This is the base color used to generate the entire color scheme.

Flutter uses this to compute all other colors (like primaryContainer, surfaceTint, etc.) in Material Design 3.

If you change this to Colors.blue, for example, the whole app will take on a bluish tone.

primary: Colors.green

This defines your primary brand color — the main color of your app.

It’s typically used for:

AppBar background

Buttons (like ElevatedButton)

Active switches, sliders, and selection controls

secondary: Colors.greenAccent

The secondary color is used for highlighting parts of your UI.

It complements the primary color and is often used for:

Floating Action Buttons

Active icons

Progress indicators

Accent details or highlights

onPrimary: Colors.white

Defines the text/icon color that appears on top of the primary color.

Since your primary color is green, text/icons over green areas (like AppBar titles or button labels) will appear white for contrast.

onSecondary: Colors.black

Defines the text/icon color that appears on top of the secondary color.

Since your secondary is light (greenAccent), black provides good contrast.
